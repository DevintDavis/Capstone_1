# Capstone 1: North Carolina Online Sales Analysis

**Year Up United | Data Analytics Training Academy**

## Project Overview

This project analyzes online sales performance for North Carolina using EmporiUm's sample sales database. The goal is to identify revenue trends, evaluate product category performance, compare North Carolina's results with other states in the South Region, and provide data-driven recommendations for improving sales performance.

## Tools & Technologies

- **MySQL Workbench:** Data querying, joins, aggregation, and analysis
- **Microsoft Excel:** PivotTables, PivotCharts, and data visualization
- **Git & GitHub:** Version control and project documentation

## Key Findings

- **Total North Carolina Online Revenue:** $346,285.31
- **Total Sales Transactions:** 386
- **Average Transaction Amount:** $897.11
- **Sales Period:** January 16, 2022 – November 11, 2025
- **Regional Ranking:** 4th among Texas, Florida, South Carolina, and North Carolina
- **Top Product Category:** Technology & Accessories, generating $254,968.35 (approximately 73.6% of North Carolina's total revenue)

An important finding was that Technology & Accessories generated $243,636.59 in August 2022 alone, indicating a significant concentration of sales during that period.

## Excel Visualizations

Two visualizations were created to support the analysis:

1. **South Region Online Sales Revenue by State:** Compares North Carolina's online revenue against Texas, Florida, and South Carolina.
2. **North Carolina Online Sales Revenue by Product Category:** Highlights the categories contributing the most revenue, with Technology & Accessories leading.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, I recommend that EmporiUm focus on strengthening North Carolina's online sales performance during the next quarter.

The company should continue supporting Technology & Accessories, its highest-revenue category, while testing targeted promotions and cross-selling opportunities to increase transactions in other product categories.

To evaluate these strategies, the company should monitor monthly transaction counts, revenue by category, and average transaction amounts.
